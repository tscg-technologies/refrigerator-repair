Symptom Guide

Common Refrigerator Problems

Identify the issue your refrigerator is presenting. Note: In refrigeration systems, symptoms indicate potential areas of concern and require physical inspection rather than immediate component replacement.

Refrigerator Not Cooling

Food spoiling, compressor running without cold air, or compartment feels warm. Potential airflow, thermostat, or coil factors.

Explore Problem →

Freezer Not Freezing

Ice cream melting, soft ice cubes, or partial freezing. Could relate to defrost cycle timing, fan circulation, or air barriers.

Explore Problem →

Excessive Ice Formation

Heavy frost over evaporator walls or drawers, often indicating humid ambient air ingress via worn gaskets or defrost faults.

Explore Problem →

Water Leakage

Puddles under the vegetable crisper or water pooling on the floor, frequently linked to blocked defrost drain tubes or drip trays.

Explore Problem →

Unusual Noises

Clicking, buzzing, scraping, or high-pitched humming from rear coils or internal fans requiring mechanical alignment check.

Explore Problem →

Refrigerator Not Starting

Complete power silence or interior light on with compressor inactive. Potential start relay, overload protector, or wiring concern.

Explore Problem →

Temperature Fluctuations

Compartments alternately freezing and warming up. Points toward thermistor inaccuracies, damper flap stickiness, or control relay drift.

Explore Problem →

Door Seal Problems

Doors popping open, condensation along exterior frames, or visible gasket cracks that allow constant warm room air penetration.

Explore Problem →
